Tishler & Wald / Selected litigation matter

I helped devise and run an unusual chancery claims process.

Tishler & Wald represented our client creditor, which asserted a blanket lien, in litigation against creditors asserting competing interests. EAR and its principals were on the opposing side of the wider dispute. EAR was never my business or employer.

I drafted the initial complaint, coordinated service and served as a contact for claimant counsel. We helped realize value from abandoned equipment. Separately, my drafting and factual work helped defeat a $17.49m competing claim. The case distinguishes my recollection from the public court record; that claim amount is not a recovery.
