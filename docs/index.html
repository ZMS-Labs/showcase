The work behind the interface

Purpose, choices,
and evidence.

Start with a consequential question.

A project needs a concrete use: understand an agreement, recover an uncertain event, improve an agent's next step, or explore a creative change.

Make the choices visible.

Interfaces express decisions about attention, uncertainty and control. The case studies explain why those arrangements matter.

Keep claims close to their evidence.

Recorded checks, implemented components and design prototypes establish different things. Each has a useful place when its status is clear.
