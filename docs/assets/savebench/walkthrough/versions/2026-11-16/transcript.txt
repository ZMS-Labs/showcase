[0:00] SaveBench. Can an AI build a working factory in Satisfactory, by the game's own rules? This is where it stands on November 16, 2026.

[0:06] The first milestone. Start in a blank world and earn milestone 1-1. It costs 100 Iron Plate, 100 Iron Rod and 200 Concrete. It must be done the way a player does it: ore comes from miners on the map's ore nodes, parts move between machines by belt, and hands are only for getting started. The game is the judge: the milestone counts when the live game accepts the payment and launches it. Two have tried. Script is a reference run, a fixed list of steps we wrote, and it is not an AI. DeepSeek is an AI model, deepseek-v4-flash, choosing every step by itself.

[0:20] The script run, run BL, November 14, shown as a diagram that is not to scale. The script clears the rock on an iron node by hand and builds a Miner Mk1 on it, powered by a Biomass Burner. Belts carry ore to the Smelter, ingots to the Constructor and plates into the bank. 324 Iron Plate arrive by belt, where 320 were wanted. The Constructor then switches to Iron Rod, and 202 arrive, where 200 were wanted. A second line starts at a Miner Mk1 on a stone node. The game refuses a belt that is too long, so the stone passes through four containers before a Constructor turns it into Concrete: 203, where 200 were wanted. No part was moved by hand between machines. The burner was still refuelled by hand, a known gap. The finished Concrete is carried to the bank once, to pay. The game accepts the payment and launches milestone 1-1. 59 checks of 59 passed. This is a script run, not an AI.

[0:46] The DeepSeek run, run 4, November 14, shown as a diagram. DeepSeek cleared the rock and built a Miner Mk1, a Smelter, a Constructor, storage, a Biomass Burner and two belts, and wired the burner to the miner. It had spent all its Iron Rod. The last belt was refused because it cost 16 Iron Plate and the bank held 1. Power poles also cost Iron Rod, so the Smelter and Constructor never got power; for the poles this is inferred. Our harness told the model only that the placement was refused, so it spent 15 of its 34 replies looking for clear ground. The run stopped at its run limit and the milestone was not earned. The fault was in our harness, not in how the model played. A fix is written and not yet live.

[1:12] How it got here. October 27: the concept video, before anything had been measured in the game. November 9: the first belt between two machines in the live game. November 12: Space Elevator phase one and the first Miner Mk1 feeding a belt. November 13: over 300 plates by belt from a miner, and the first Tractor built. November 14: milestone 1-1 by a real factory, the first copper Wire and the first closed vehicle loop. Game actions checked in the live game rose from 5 of 51 on November 9 to 28 of 51 on November 11, 29 of 52 on November 13 and 38 of 52 on November 14. [1:20] What is still ahead. Milestone 1-2 by a real factory needs 150 Iron Plate, 150 Iron Rod and 300 Wire; the run is packaged and waiting to launch. A Tractor, two stations and a closed loop are built, but the game still refuses to let the Tractor drive and the cause is not known. No AI model has finished a milestone; DeepSeek's next run waits for the fix that tells it when it cannot afford a build. Beyond that lies the whole game: 141 buildings, and the aim is all of them.
