SaveBench · Game-grounded factory evaluation

Test the measuring instrument before judging the contestant.

SaveBench evaluates AI-authored factories in Satisfactory using the actual game. In this experiment, a known-working control reported zero delivery. That was a reason to stop judging the factory and investigate the instrument.

From an invalid instrument to a valid failing result.

These authored diagrams and tables explain two retained historical trials. They display recorded results; they do not run the game or simulate a new contestant.

Step 1 of 5

The known-flow control reports zero.

The control fixture should deliver both products. Instead, it records zero copper ingots and zero concrete in each of two 61-second game windows.

The trial stops as INCONCLUSIVE before obtaining a contestant game observation. Its contestant measurements remain absent, rather than becoming a zero-production score.

Repair the instrument, then test both directions.

The investigation found an inventory-filter defect in the save-building adapter. Its encoding was corrected against game-written containers.

In the new trial, the known-flow control delivers both materials. Removing the connection yields zero for both. Positive delivery and an expected zero now provide complementary checks.

Keep the design and targets. Disclose the rebuild.

The design, task and generation manifest are byte-identical across the two trials. The design revision and challenge identity also match.

The compiled save bytes are different. The adapter and agent-tool implementation changed, the instrument identity changed, and the candidate was rebuilt. This is a repaired evaluation of the same design—not a comparison using identical binaries or an unchanged instrument.

Judge every window against both targets.

The factory delivers copper ingots and concrete. But the rule requires at least 20 copper ingots/min and 15 concrete/min in every measured window.

Copper misses in the first window. Both products meet their targets in the second. Concrete misses in the third. Later copper production does not cancel an earlier miss.

Retain the miss instead of moving the target.

The repaired trial is scored FAIL against the frozen per-window targets. The earlier INCONCLUSIVE attempt remains a separate record.

The failing result is useful precisely because it is specific: the factory produced both materials, but it did not satisfy every required delivery window. It is not a general statement about the model or the factory’s entire operating life.

The evidence supports the decision that was actually made. No invented aggregate score, relaxed threshold or current model-performance claim is added to this historical experiment.

Make a valid failure possible.

Qualify the measuring instrument first.

Choice
Run known-flow and disconnected controls before interpreting contestant delivery.
Reason
A detector that cannot see expected production cannot distinguish a poor design from its own failure.
Evidence
The first trial retains missing contestant measurements and an inconclusive outcome.

Preserve what the contestant was asked to do.

Choice
Carry the identical design and task into a new evaluation after repairing the instrument.
Reason
Changing the target or quietly editing the factory would answer a different question.
Boundary
The repaired adapter rebuilds the save. Those bytes and the instrument identity change and are recorded as such.

Score the declared rule, not an attractive average.

Choice
Require both output minima in every window.
Reason
A burst of later production cannot conceal a delivery shortfall in another required interval.
Evidence
The recorded verdict remains a failure despite production of both materials and a passing middle window.

Purpose and contribution.

Personal direction
Defined the benchmark around interesting factory-design choices, competing goals and outcomes demonstrated by the actual game. Later planning explicitly refocused the project on factory design rather than merely discovering automation.
Work shown here
An existing experiment’s instrument repair, controls and retained result. Development used AI assistance; this case does not establish sole personal authorship of the adapter, evaluator or tests.

A historical result with a precise boundary.

The case was checked against retained trial reports and game-observation verdicts from 9 September 2026. The measurement summary preserves the exact numeric rates, targets and per-window outcomes.

Checked during preparation
Control outcomes and rates; the absent first-trial contestant observation; byte-identical design, task and manifest; changed candidate and instrument identities; and a recomputation of each window’s target result.
Not rerun for this page
No new game session, model contest, cluster action or paid trial. This page does not claim a fresh validation of today’s implementation.

What this experiment does not establish

It is not a model ranking, a completed autonomous progression campaign or a claim that every supported factory design works. It does not measure long-run performance outside the recorded windows.

The introductory diagram and result tables are authored explanations of retained evidence. No game screenshot, map tile, save file, application source or private operational detail is published here.
