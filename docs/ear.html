Experience / Tishler & Wald

Tishler & Wald · Litigation

One court process for more than 100 competing claims.

After Equipment Acquisition Resources filed for bankruptcy in 2009, it abandoned equipment claimed by more than 100 lenders and leasing companies. Our creditor client claimed a security interest in all its assets. Other parties asserted rights to particular equipment.

As part of the firm's team, I co-designed and ran a claims process in Cook County chancery court, outside the bankruptcy case. I drafted the complaint, coordinated service, worked with claimants' lawyers, and kept changing asset and claim records organized. Professional auctioneers handled the inventory and sale. The court authorized an auction, with claims attaching to the proceeds.

I also drafted the central trial-court motion against a $17,487,520.30 competing claim. The creditor had acquired real estate through a full credit bid that satisfied the same debt. The trial court granted summary judgment in March 2012. The Illinois Appellate Court affirmed in September 2013; further review was denied in January 2014. The figure is the claim defeated, not money recovered.
