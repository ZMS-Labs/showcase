A separate example records two actual component checks on synthetic clauses. Those checks did not produce the graph, review items or decisions in the prototype images.

Krewcible

The captures and silent recording show existing checkpoint-editor controls and composition logic in an isolated study with synthetic state. The controls changed the modification stack and its deterministic text. They did not invoke a generation service or prove persistent product storage. The recording's composition-output panel is study instrumentation displaying the existing deterministic text function.

Fleet Orchestrator

Three isolated control-plane tests passed using synthetic fixtures, real HTTP handlers, in-memory state and a no-op launcher. The guided view summarizes assertions across related tests. Eligibility to proceed is not completion, and these tests do not demonstrate production authentication, durable persistence or live agent operation.

Design and type provenance.

The showcase extends the existing ZMS Labs type and colour direction. Its layouts were prepared with AI assistance. The Gridiron and Epistemic Skills introductory illustrations were generated with OpenAI image generation, then reviewed for relevance and clarity. They are conceptual artwork, not screenshots, measured results or an architecture specification. Their labels and explanations remain selectable page text. The product images preserve the corresponding project's own design language.

Archivo is bundled unmodified under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts. Steno's prototype renders use Source Serif 4, Inter and IBM Plex Mono. Krewcible's study uses Instrument Serif, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono. The product fonts are present only as rendered pixels here; font provenance and original notices are retained with the private preparation records.

No real agreement, matter record, portrait, game capture, generated model asset or model weight is included in the presentation. Images, recordings and captions identify synthetic and illustrative content. Both silent recordings have descriptive text tracks and selectable transcripts; they preserve real interactions without generated demonstration frames.

Source and distribution.

The existing Showcase source is distributed under its GPL license. The Gridiron archive retains its own GPL-3.0-or-later declarations and notices. Third-party components retain their own terms.

Steno, Krewcible and Fleet implementation source is not distributed by these case studies. Their selected artifacts and explanatory material do not grant access to the private applications. This site is served by GitHub Pages and also runs locally as static files; it contains no analytics, forms, model calls or backend integration.
