Design and type provenance.

The showcase extends the existing ZMS Labs type and colour direction. Its layouts were prepared with AI assistance. The Gridiron and Epistemic Skills introductory illustrations were generated with OpenAI image generation, then reviewed for relevance and clarity. They are conceptual artwork, not screenshots, measured results or an architecture specification. Their labels and explanations remain selectable page text. The product images preserve the corresponding project's own design language.

Archivo is bundled unmodified under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts. Steno's prototype renders use Source Serif 4, Inter and IBM Plex Mono. Krewcible's study uses Instrument Serif, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono. Fleet Orchestrator uses its source-defined system-font fallback for these offline specimens. Neuraxic uses its declared local font fallbacks in the existing reduced-motion layout. The product fonts are present only as rendered pixels here; font provenance and original notices are retained with the private preparation records.

No real agreement, matter record, portrait, game capture, generated model asset or model weight is included in the presentation. Images, recordings and captions identify synthetic and illustrative content. All four silent recordings have descriptive text tracks and selectable transcripts; they preserve real interactions without generated demonstration frames.
