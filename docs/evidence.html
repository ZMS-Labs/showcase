Who made what.

I directed the page layouts and typography, with AI tools carrying out the design and build. The site's typeface is Archivo, bundled unmodified under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts. Product screenshots keep each product's own look and fonts, which appear here only as pixels: Source Serif 4, Inter and IBM Plex Mono in Steno's prototype images, and Instrument Serif, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono in Krewcible's study. Fleet Orchestrator's and Neuraxic's images use the fallback fonts their own code specifies. Where each font came from, and its original notice, are kept with the private preparation records.

The Gridiron and Epistemic Skills illustrations were made with OpenAI image generation and are labeled as AI-generated artwork wherever they appear. They contain no screenshots or measured results.

All four recordings are silent captures of the real interfaces with made-up content, with captions and selectable transcripts.
