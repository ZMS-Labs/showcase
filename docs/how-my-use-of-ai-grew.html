How my use of AI grew

My use of AI began in 2022. I received a DALL-E invite in June to July 2022 and started using the OpenAI API in December 2022.

ChatGPT Plus followed in February 2023, the GPT-4 API in July 2023, and GitHub Copilot in December 2023. I opened my first Claude account in April 2025. In January 2026, I began using Claude Code and Claude Pro.

In 2026, I wanted to see how far I could follow an idea: work through the options, have something built, try it, and come back with a more specific request. AI tools wrote the code. My part was deciding what I wanted, understanding the choices, and checking what came back.

There were times when work developed around an assumption that should have been checked first. I wanted better ways to catch those problems before more work depended on them. The rules evolved around permission, evidence, recovery, records and confidentiality. My own design had to be open to revision, too.

I found different uses for different assistants: exploring an idea with one, having another implement it, and asking a third to challenge the result. I have carried plenty of those handoffs between sessions myself.

Under ZMS Labs, Fleet Orchestrator is where I want that experience to come together: one place to see the work, direct the next step and carry decisions between sessions. Animated characters, persistent sessions and model handoffs are parts of the project. Making the whole experience work smoothly is still the ambition.
