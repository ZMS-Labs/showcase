Dovenmuehle / Commercial agreements

I made the terms usable
for the planned work.

My in-house practice centered on commercial agreements. I reviewed, drafted and negotiated the documents, explained how their terms affected the planned work, and helped colleagues understand the issues and available options.

Understand the terms in context

The document needs to fit
the work people plan to do.

I worked across master services agreements, statements of work, confidentiality and data-protection terms, riders and related commercial documents. I explained issues such as liability, indemnity, intellectual property, service commitments and termination in relation to the planned work.

That meant working with business, technical, oversight and compliance colleagues and leadership. I also addressed audit rights, insurance, subcontracting and continuity. My role was to explain the terms and available options, with business decisions and technical validation owned by the appropriate colleagues.

Creation / drafting / active use

I created a rider and
a negotiation reference.

I created the contract rider. I also created and actively used a negotiation reference that recorded preferred positions, fallback language and reasoning.

The reference helped me keep a position connected to why it matters and what alternatives may be workable. The draft below illustrates the structure of that kind of thinking; it does not reconstruct an actual company term.

Documents and repeatable processes

I developed NDA documents
and ways to use them.

I developed NDA documents and intake, review and signature processes. I also created a repeatable assurance-report access process by coordinating the participants, required documents and routing.

The particular tri-party NDA and indemnity side-letter used in that access process already existed. My contribution there was making the process repeatable; I did not originate those forms.

Experience

Commercial and technology agreements.

I worked at Dovenmuehle from February 2017 through October 2026: Staff Attorney, then Corporate Counsel from January 2020. The title change did not materially change my responsibilities. Another lawyer owned customer contracting; my support in that area was narrower.
