Purpose · authorship · judgment

Make difficult work
easier to examine.

ZMS Labs brings together personally directed projects in contract analysis, applied reasoning, software experimentation and creative tools. The recurring question is practical: what should a person be able to see, understand and decide?

The thread across the projects.

Steno puts a clause beside the questions it raises. Gridiron separates an observed event from language about it. Krewcible makes creative choices editable. Epistemic Skills asks agents to choose useful methods and check their outcomes. Fleet Orchestrator examines when a workflow is allowed to proceed. SaveBench tests the instrument behind a result, Neuraxic separates alternate continuity from canon, ZMS Canvas keeps edits recoverable when a save fails, and Enaction makes fictional-role attribution explicit.

These are different domains with a shared concern: making important relationships, changes and uncertainties easier to inspect.

A clear account of the work.

Personal direction
Problem selection, product intent, workflow priorities, tradeoffs and the standard for what the public presentation should demonstrate. The case studies make these choices concrete.
AI-assisted construction
Implementation, investigation, documentation, visual preparation and verification use AI assistance. The portfolio does not imply that every line of code or visual detail was manually authored.
Upstream work
Libraries, fonts, tools and forked projects retain their authorship and notices. Supporting forks are identified separately from original project direction.
What a result means
A prototype demonstrates a design. A focused test demonstrates specified behavior under its conditions. Neither automatically establishes a finished, deployed product.
